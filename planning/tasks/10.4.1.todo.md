# 10.4.1 — Collect the R queue in one submodule

## Goal
Move the R queue code out of `crates/engine/src/cmd/gp.rs` into `crates/engine/src/cmd/gp_r.rs`,
gated once at the `mod` line. Behaviour-preserving. Get `grep -c 'feature = "r"' crates/engine/src/cmd/gp.rs` to 5 or fewer.
